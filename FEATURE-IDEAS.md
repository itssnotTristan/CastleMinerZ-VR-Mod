# Possible future additions

These are ideas, not features shipped in v0.60.

- **Co-op blood moon events:** a shared countdown, escalating waves, and a group reward, with the host choosing whether events run.
- **Building blueprints:** save a small player-built structure, preview its placement, and show the materials needed before constructing it.
- **VR backpack and tool belt:** optional physical inventory access alongside the normal menus, with seated reach adjustment.
- **World landmarks and expeditions:** discoverable outposts and optional distance-based objectives that give co-op groups a reason to travel together.
- **Avatar mirror and fit preview:** check a body, head turn, reach, and two-hand grip before joining a server.

Original gameplay fixes, multiplayer reliability, and stable headset frame rate take priority over these additions.
